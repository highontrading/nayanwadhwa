About

At the intersection of quantitative derivatives trading and agentic AI.

I specialize in quantitative research, options pricing modeling, and mid-frequency derivatives trading metrics. Through Ideabro AI and Ideabro Studio, I construct autonomous multi-agent software networks and high-fidelity DTC fashion ad generation pipelines.

I’m also co-founder at Attention Labs, a content-led growth agency that builds owned media for founders, funds and brands.

Quick answers

Asked often.

Are you open to AI or options consulting?

Yes. I’m available for selective AI agency clients, custom SaaS automation projects, and options trading consulting. I run Ideabro AI and Ideabro Studio from Delhi / Gurugram, India. Send me a note and we can take it from there.

What does Ideabro AI do?

Ideabro AI builds multi-agent workflow systems, custom LLM routing architectures, and scalable business automations for enterprises. Ideabro Studio is its subsidiary, delivering model-led visual campaigns for apparel, jewelry, and luxury retail brands.

What is Attention Labs?

A content-led growth agency I co-founded. We build owned media for founders, funds and brands: podcasts, newsletters and YouTube channels that rank, get shared and generate demand. More at attentionlabs.com.

How do your derivatives pricing models work?

The Quant Pricing Engine models options with Black-Scholes formulas and binomial lattice projections. It computes option prices and the dynamic hedging Greeks (Delta, Gamma, Vega, Theta), and interpolates implied volatility skews. You can try the Black-Scholes pricer on this page.

What is the Derive Trading YouTube channel?

My educational channel on quantitative options theory, premium writing strategies, risk controls, and market mechanics. It runs from basic credit spreads and iron condors up to multi-leg volatility arbitrage and backtests.
